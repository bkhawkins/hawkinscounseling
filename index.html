Meet B. Keith Hawkins, DCCC

As a Licensed Clinical Pastoral Counselor with the NCCA, I am dedicated to providing a safe, confidential, and professional environment for individuals and families to navigate life's challenges with faith and purpose.

My approach is grounded in the belief that every person is uniquely created. By integrating proven clinical temperament therapy with sound Biblical principles, we work together toward wholeness and renewed hope.
