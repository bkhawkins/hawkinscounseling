Free 6-Day Devotional

With the Shepherd

Two Perspectives on Psalm 23: The Shepherd and the Sheep

A free 6-day devotional — the companion to the Bible study. One verse of Psalm 23 a day, read from both sides: what the Shepherd is doing, and what the sheep is learning to trust.

Send Me the Devotional

Free PDF · 5 pages · Yours to keep and share

What's Inside

Each day takes one verse of Psalm 23 and includes a short reflection, a prayer, and a journal prompt.

1

Mine

Psalm 23:1 — Belonging before benefits: He is mine, and I am His.

2

Made to Rest

Psalm 23:2 — Receiving rest in green pastures, beside still waters.

3

Restored, for His Name's Sake

Psalm 23:3 — Being found, restored, and led for His name, not our record.

4

Through

Psalm 23:4 — Walking through the valley, where the grass grows and He is with us.

5

The Table

Psalm 23:5 — Eating at His table in the presence of our enemies.

6

Pursued by Mercy

Psalm 23:6 — Goodness and mercy after us all our days — He is our reward, again and again.

From the Bible Study

"Sheep are made to be led, not driven. The Shepherd goes ahead, calls clearly, and keeps gathering the flock close — and the sheep's whole safety is in staying near His voice."
