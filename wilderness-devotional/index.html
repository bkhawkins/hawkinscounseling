Free 7-Day Devotional

Lead Me to the Wilderness

When God Uses the Wasteland to Save Us

A free 7-day personal prayer & journaling guide — the companion to the 4-part Bible study. Reframe your wilderness season, release panic, and learn to listen for God's voice in the dry places.

Send Me the Devotional

Free PDF · 10 pages · Yours to keep and share

What's Inside

Each day includes a Scripture passage, a short reflection, two journaling prompts with space to write, and a prayer to pray aloud.

1

Pinned in the Bottleneck

Exodus 14:1–3 — Recognizing where God has brought you.

2

Surrounded by the Enemy's Watchtowers

Exodus 14:4 & Psalm 121 — Facing the exposure of Migdol and Baal-zephon.

3

The Lure of Predictable Bondage

Exodus 14:10–12 — Confronting the temptation to run back to Egypt.

4

Standing Still at the Sea

Exodus 14:13–14 — Releasing frantic striving at Yam Suph.

5

The Classroom of Dependence

Deuteronomy 8 & Matthew 4 — Learning that man does not live by bread alone.

6

The Voice in the Wasteland

Hosea 2:14–15 & Isaiah 43 — Hearing God speak tenderly in the desert.

7

Today, Do Not Harden Your Heart

Hebrews 3–4 — Crossing into God's promised rest.

From the 4-Part Bible Study

"The wilderness is not your cemetery; it is your birthplace. God brings us into the desert to bury our past, break our reliance on Egypt's false security, and reveal His saving power."
