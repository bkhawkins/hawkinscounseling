Free 6-Day Devotional

Let This Mind Be in You

The Mind of Christ — When "Be Filled with My Spirit" Meant a New Mindset

A free 6-day devotional — the companion to the Bible study. A man of God, filled and faithful for decades, kept hearing the Lord say, "Be filled with My Spirit" — until his understanding opened: the Lord was talking about his mindset. One theme a day on the renewed mind.

Send Me the Devotional

Free PDF · Yours to keep and share

What's Inside

Each day takes one theme from the study and includes a short reflection, a prayer, and a journal prompt.

1

Filled, Again

Ephesians 5:18 — "Keep on being filled": the filling is a posture, not a memory.

2

Already Yours

1 Corinthians 2:16 — We have the mind of Christ; learning to listen for it.

3

The Servant's Mind

Philippians 2:5–8 — Less grasping, more serving; trusting the Father with the outcome.

4

Renewed

Romans 12:2 — Transformation by the renewing of the mind — the heart of cognitive counseling.

5

Stayed

Isaiah 26:3 — Perfect peace for the mind that stays on Him.

6

Every Thought Captive

2 Corinthians 10:5 — Guarding the gate: not every thought deserves to stay.

From the Bible Study

"The filling isn't only about how much of the Spirit we have. It's about how much of our mindset He has."
